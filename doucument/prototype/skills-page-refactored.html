Method, source and limitations

How this prototype works: confirmed task wording is mapped to the WEF Future of Jobs 2025 core skills list using transparent matching rules. A skill appears only when at least one task supports it.

Employer percentages are global reference data, not a personal proficiency score. GenAI capacity describes parts of a skill group that may be supported by AI; it does not mean that a person or occupation will be replaced.
